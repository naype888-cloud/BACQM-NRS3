Stop 3 of 9 · Where does a region keep its information?

Holography — a region keeps its information on its walls

The entropy of a region is counted on its boundary: every step across the wall is one bit, open or closed. On the open cube a cut has one wall, dy·dz steps. On the three-torus every axis closes on itself, so the same cut has two walls, the plane and the seam: 2·dy·dz steps and S = 2·dy·dz·log 2, whatever the depth.

What is exact here

  • Lean The cut of T³ has 2·dy·dz steps and S = 2·dy·dz·log 2, independent of the depth and of the plane.
  • Lean With one bit of area per step, S = A/(4ℓ_P²) exactly when a² = 4ℓ_P² log 2.
  • Lean The open cube has one wall, dy·dz steps, and the same bit of area.
