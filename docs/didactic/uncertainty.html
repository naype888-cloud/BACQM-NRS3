Stop 1 of 9 · Why can't position and transport both be sharp on a closed axis?

Position and transport on a closed axis — every torus has its star

On the three-torus every axis is closed: the last position is linked to the first. Each axis carries a position P and a transport T°, and the two do not commute, so no state makes both sharp. Robertson's 1934 inequality says how much: the determinant of the fluctuations is at least the determinant of the brackets. With three positions per axis, at the maximal current state the ratio is 125/64. Pick a torus, run the transport, and drag either panel to turn both.

The angle at c on every ring

At the maximal current state every closed axis opens an angle θ, never zero: the dark share is positive. With 3 positions it is exactly arccos(2/√5) = 26.57° (light 4/5, Lean); with 4 or more positions it is computed here, between about 45° and 50°: the browser finds c as the top eigenvector of K = i[T°, P] (Jacobi diagonalization, double precision) and reads the angle from it. ○ θ at c · ● θ at Ψ(t). The page runs on its own: the transport bar fills once, then a new random torus, and the bar fills again. Press Tour to stop on a torus, Transport to pause.

The octahedron of δ

drag to turn · dashed: the largest δ

Each closed axis with d positions carries δ(d) = 1/cos θ − 1 at c. Put a vertex at ±δ on every axis: the octahedron of the torus. The canonical 3 × 3 × 3 gives the smallest, δ(3) = √5/2 − 1 ≈ 0.11803 on every axis (exact). Its volume is (4/3)·𝒱, with 𝒱 = δ(dx) δ(dy) δ(dz); the values for 4 or more positions are computed here.

Tension and the angle

|t(t)| against θ(t), one panel per axis

The tension of an axis is t = ⟨K⟩, K = i[T°, P]. In the band |t| > 1 every state keeps a positive dark share (TorusDark.darkShare_pos_of_band) and det|NRS³| is strict on T³ for every state with the three axes in the band (TorusStrict.robertson_det_torus_lt). The maximal tension is the state c: √6/2 with 3 positions (ClosedThree.tension_maxState); for every d it lies in [1, 1 + 2/(d − 1)] and tends to 1 as the ring grows (ClosedAxis.one_le_cMax, ClosedAxis.cMax_le, ClosedAxis.tendsto_cMax): the current concentrates on the seam. Press Transport: Ψ(t) starts at c and leaves it.

Position by position: where does the probability go?

Ask a yes/no question: "is the excitation at position j?" Its chance is p_j (the violet disc). When the transport runs, that chance can only change by flowing through the two links of j (the orange arrows), like water between neighbouring tanks: what one position loses, its neighbour gains, and the total never changes. And there is a speed limit for each question: a position that is surely full, or surely empty, is not changing at that moment (the dashed frames). Press Transport; then try a state of definite energy.

What is exact here

  • Lean For every state on the torus, det Σ ≥ |det Ω|.
  • Lean With the three axes in their band the inequality is strict, for every unit state, entangled or not.
  • Lean At the maximal current state with three positions per axis, det Σ = (5/4)³ |det Ω| = 125/64 |det Ω|; the ladder open cube 1, box 5/4, T³ 125/64, T⁴ 625/256.
  • Lean Position by position: the rate of each position is the flow through its links, the rates add up to zero, the flows add up to the velocity, and each rate is bounded by Robertson for a yes/no question, on R³, T³, the fibred box and T³ × w.
  • Browser With four or more positions per axis the numbers on this page are numerical, computed from the same matrices; the ring of positions is computed from its modes.
