Stop 8 of 9 · How does a cut give the Einstein equation?

From a cut to the Einstein equation

Every cut carries one bit per step, so its entropy per unit area is η = log 2 / a², the same for every cut, on T³ and on the open cube. With one bit of area, a² = 4ℓ_P² log 2, this is η = 1/(4ℓ_P²). An accelerated observer sees the Unruh temperature; asking the Clausius relation δQ = T dS on every light sheet then gives R_kk = 8πℓ_P² T_kk, and with the Bianchi identity the Einstein equation. Move the area per step: only the bit of area gives 8π.
