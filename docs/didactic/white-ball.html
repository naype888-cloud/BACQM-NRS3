The ball of yarn — why a star that does not collapse shines on its face

Everything you see is a field that has been excited and is being measured; the photon is the carrier that brings it to your eye. Think of bubble wrap. The plastic that joins the bubbles is the hopping, the transport that moves the excitations. Each bubble is one quantum of RS^N³, and it is transparent: the thin film around it is its boundary, and what fills it is a probability. Behind a transparent bubble you see black only where nothing has happened: there is nothing there to measure.

A star whose core stays below its collapse mass spreads its matter over many quanta and fills none of them: it is the sheet crumpled into a ball without popping a single bubble, layers on layers, and from outside you see only light on its face. Keep stacking layers and the face fills up: the bubble pops and the quantum turns dark. That is the inverted quantum — the same black as the background, but full instead of empty, and still a quantum, never a point.

The ball: threads, layers and the lit face

Dots: the hopping state |Ψ|² on every position. Clear walls: the layers. Front wall: the face, one LED per step. Drag to turn.

The core mass decides the colour

Below 2.08 M☉ the face shines; from 3.2 M☉ the quantum is full and dark. In between, the measured stars do not decide where the collapse mass is.

What is exact here

  • Lean Every face of the outer wall hides the same number of interiors, 2^(layers·dy·dz): from outside you cannot tell what is inside.
  • Lean Spread over the quanta with none full, every layer keeps light; one full layer is one dark quantum.
  • Lean Pop the whole sheet and the face is popped: a full ball shows a full face. So a face that still shines hides a ball that is not full.
  • Declared bridge Each layer of light adds a little to what the face holds, until it is full. Then a fixed number of layers, the face's capacity divided by that little, is enough to darken it — on the open cube, on T³ and on the fibred box. The dark face has every step open half of the time: nothing stands out.
  • Lean A full quantum is still a quantum, not a point: fixing the position never stops the transport.
  • Lean The Chandrasekhar mass from CODATA 2018 and IAU 2015 lies between 1.43 and 1.44 M☉.
  • Declared bridge A quantum below capacity shines on its edge, a full one is dark; the core fills its quantum at the collapse mass, somewhere between 2.08 and 3.2 M☉.
  • Browser The brightness of each LED is |Ψ|² at the two ends of its step; the drawing and the colours are the page's, not theorems.
