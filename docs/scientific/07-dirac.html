B7 · Dirac operator / doubler modes

On T³ = ZMod a × ZMod b × ZMod c with even sizes put the closed transports T_x, T_y, T_z and the Kogut–Susskind signs A_y = (−1)^x T_y, A_z = (−1)^(x+y) T_z, ε = (−1)^(x+y+z). The massive operator is D_m = T_x + A_y + A_z + m ε. On T³ × w the fibre of three positions takes the place of the mass, D₄ = T_x + A_y + A_z + M_w with M_w = ε T°_w: its modes give the mass ½, a gap |E| ≥ ½ and, because the axes are closed, a speed limit 1/φ.

The eight doublers, spectral side: hopping, Dirac and their sum

Everything here is the torus above, at the same time: its modes, the occupied mode (○), Ψ(s) (●, at ⟨cos⟩ on each axis, at ⟨cos²⟩ in the squares) and the turn. Every mode (a, b, c) of the torus sits at (cos θa, cos θb, cos θc) in the cube of cosines. The hopping E = −2t Σ cos vanishes on a whole plane, Σ cos = 0. With the Kogut–Susskind signs the three axes anticommute and the energy becomes a length, E = ±√(cos²θa + cos²θb + cos²θc + m²): it vanishes at one point only, the origin, where the eight doublers (±π/2)³ sit. The square root turns the zero plane of the hopping into the zero point of Dirac. Below, their sum: in the squares (cos², cos², cos²) the Dirac energy squared is the sum of the three axes, D² = Tx² + Ty² + Tz² + m². The fibre of 3 positions gives the mass ½ (TorusFiberDirac.dirac_tensor): the origin opens into the gap |E| ≥ ½.

Hopping: E = −2t Σ cos, zero on a plane

Dirac: E = ±√(Σ cos² + m²), zero at one point

Lean (nrs3-tritoro): TorusDirac3.dirac3_sq (Dm² = Tx² + Ty² + Tz² + m² on T³, even sizes), the anticommutations Tx_Ay, Tx_Az, Ay_Az, eps_Tx, eps_Ay, eps_Az, and dirac3_wave (the eight waves (±i)x(±i)y(±i)z are zero modes of D₀, and the mass sends each to the opposite corner). The spectrum of each ring is the Lean TorusSpectrum.eq_cos_of_eigenvector. The fibre as fourth axis is TorusFiberDirac.dirac_sq; its rotating modes give the mass ½ and the gap (TorusFiberDirac.dirac_doublers, TorusFiberDirac.abs_energy_ge).

The mass gap and the speed limit

One closed axis at momentum k: the transport is cos k, the energy E = ±√(cos²k + m²) and the velocity of each eigenstate is the slope of its band. No eigenvalue enters |E| < m, and no slope exceeds r = √(1 + m²) − m; the orange points reach it, at cos²k = m r. Move over a panel to follow one state. On T³ × w the same 1/φ holds on each axis for every eigenvector.

The hopping band of every ring

Each ring of d positions has hopping eigenvalues −2t cos(2πm/d), m = 0, …, d − 1. They fill [−2|t|, 2|t|] and touch both ends: m = 0 gives −2t, and for even d the alternating mode gives +2t. ○ the occupied mode · ● ⟨H⟩ of Ψ(s), with the spread σH. At s = 0 the state is one site and ⟨H⟩ = 0 with the spread open. At s = 1 the dot sits on the mode and the spread closes. Press Tour to stay on one torus, Hop to pause.

The octahedron of the band

Put a vertex at ±1 on every axis: that octahedron is the whole cosine band, |λ| ≤ 1 on each ring, the same bound for every size (TorusSpeed.abs_energy_le_one). The inner octahedron has vertices at the occupied |cos(2πm/d)|. On the canonical 3 × 3 × 3 the modes are 1 and −1/2, so a single axis occupies either the outer vertex or 1/2.
