B3 · Boundary entropy / area law

Let crossSteps dx dy dz c be the pairs (p, q) with p.x ≤ c < q.x and T°_pq ≠ 0. On T³ it is the disjoint union of planeSteps (x = c | c + 1) and seamSteps (x = dx − 1 | 0). The entropy of the cut is log |𝒫(crossSteps)|.
