B5 · Bakry–Émery curvature / Ricci lower bound

For f on the sites, Γ(f) and Γ₂(f) are the carré du champ and its iterate for the adjacency of T³. A curvature bound K means Γ₂(f) ≥ K Γ(f) for every f.
