B6 · Lorentz cone / admissible cone

On T^k (closed axes of three positions), for an axis with centered vectors x, y of T° and P, the Gram matrix G = [[‖x‖², ⟨x,y⟩], [⟨y,x⟩, ‖y‖²]] is Hermitian, G = v₀1 + v₁σ₁ + v₂σ₂ + v₃σ₃, and v = axisVector Φ i (UncertaintyCone.coneVector). The Minkowski interval of v is det G.

The speed ball: 𝐯 / v₀ inside the unit sphere

drag to turn

The sphere is the light cone seen at v₀ = 1: its surface is β = 1. Grey: where the vector started; orange: where M sends it; the trail follows the sliders. It can get close to the sphere, never through it.

The hyperbola: v₀² − |𝐯|² = det G

Every M moves the point along the same hyperbola; the light line |𝐯| = v₀ is its asymptote. A null vector (the saturating state of the closed axis, d = 3) lives on the light line and stays there.
